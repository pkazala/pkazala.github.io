This is the first small note on the redesigned site.

The idea is simple: each post is a markdown file in `src/content/blog` with a little frontmatter at the top. Astro turns it into a page automatically, and the blog index picks it up without any extra wiring.

Future posts can be technical notes, photography reflections, or small records of things worth remembering.
